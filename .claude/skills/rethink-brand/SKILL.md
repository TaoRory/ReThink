# ReThink brand and facts

## Brand identity (KV AY 26-27 — `../KV/New KV/`)
- **Concept**: the pendulum / Newton's cradle. Four beats — `Default Orbit → Encounter → Pause → Choice`. ReThink's role is to create the *intersection* and the *pause*, never to force the change. Full write-up: `../KV/New KV/ReThink AY26-27 _ Concept Elaboration.docx`.
- **Logotype**: monoline uppercase "RETHINK" as a neon tube (white core, fuchsia/violet bloom), with a "26-27" season tag. No script/calligraphic initial — the AY 25-26 script "Re" is retired.
- **Palette** (sampled from the KV PNGs): near-black `#04060c` ground; spectrum fuchsia `#d633e8` → violet `#7a15f0` → azure `#2540d0` → cyan `#4ab8dd`/`#70e0f0`; pearl highlight `#e3ccff`; deep wine halo `#5c1830`.
- **Motifs**: suspended glass spheres on hairlines, crossing beams (Encounter), halos, a lit pearl, **square** light motes (the 4-point sparkle ✦ is retired — use `▪`), fine film grain.
- **Typography**: display = Lexend (geometric monoline, wide tracking, light weights); body = Be Vietnam Pro. Both carry full Vietnamese diacritics.
- **Voice**: "Rethink & Let Rethink · Embrace Diversity · Own & Act Together · Reflect to Evolve". Since 2022.
- `../KV/RETHINK BOOKLET AY 25-26/` is the **previous** KV, kept for reference only.
- **Exception: `/halloween` is deliberately NOT in this KV.** It follows the Halloween KV (`../KV/kv halloween - ReThink/`), scoped under `.hlw`. Do not apply the fuchsia/violet palette, neon tubing or the cradle there, and do not leak `.hlw` styles out. Details: the `halloween-kv` skill.
- The "TEDx" word is always **TED red neon** (`.text-neon-red`, `#e62b1e`), never cyan, and the name is written **"TEDx VinUniversity"** (with the space).
